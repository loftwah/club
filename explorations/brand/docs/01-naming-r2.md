# Phase 2 (Round 2) — Naming Research

> **Date:** 2026-08-23 (round 2)
> **Replaces:** `01-naming.md` is retained as a previous hypothesis, not as a final list.
> **Status:** Phase 2 · decision package (revised exploration)

The previous round (`01-naming.md`) generated 28 names biased toward
"heritage society" register. That bias is acknowledged in the user's
direction and corrected here.

The actual product is about creating a very specific emotional
experience: being wanted, being included, having plans involving you,
being remembered, being sent things, your birthday mattering, your
membership having continuity, your social calendar appearing alive,
having social credibility, not having to perform socially, the
obligation eventually disappearing, the relief of staying home
without feeling unwanted or forgotten. The institution exists to
make that experience coherent and believable.

Names should make a prospective member think:

> "This is weirdly brilliant. This feels made for me. I actually
> want to belong to this."

Not:

> "This looks like an expensive fictional society."

This round generates **at least 35 serious candidates total**,
explores semantic territory around belonging, invitations, plans,
being expected, being remembered, having company, social life,
social credibility, connection, correspondence, relief, cancellation,
absence, availability, membership, familiarity, and personal
attention, and refreshes the shortlist.

I do **not** claim formal trademark clearance. This is creative and
SEO research only.

---

## 1. What changed since the previous round

The previous round scored heavily on:

- institutional credibility;
- stationery / seal quality;
- spoken quality;
- lore potential;
- SEO collision;
- dedicated-domain potential.

The current round also scores on:

- emotional fit (the customer experience, not the institution);
- contemporary register (avoiding the "whisky brand / law firm /
  menswear / heritage bank" trap);
- memorability for a _new_ audience, not a Victorian-fantasy audience;
- flexibility across digital and physical channels;
- subtle humour (the dry joke is welcome, the explained joke is not);
- the "social choreography" (the fact that the product _manufactures_
  social plans and then cancels them).

Names that scored well in the previous round on _institutional_ criteria
but score poorly on the _emotional_ criteria are flagged. Some
survive; some do not.

## 3. New candidates (round 2)

The new round explores semantic territory that the previous round did
not enter. The territory is the **customer experience** rather than the
institution, and the **calendar** rather than the seal.

Names are grouped by the emotional territory they come from. They are
not ranked within the group; the shortlist section ranks across
groups.

### 3.1 Belonging + plans + being expected (the "you're on the list" emotional core)

29. **Plans With You** — the product in three words. The plans _include_
    you. The brand is the verb, not the noun.
30. **Plans For You** — same emotional core, slightly more intimate.
31. **The Standing Date** — a date that is always there. Strong
    re-cast of the inherited "Standing Invitation".
32. **The Held Table** — your seat is held. Restaurant and
    theatre code; warm.
33. **Held For You** — short, soft, almost a phrase on a card.
34. **The Always-Invited** — you are always invited. The kindness
    is in the always.
35. **The Always List** — you are on the always-list; a list that
    is not a waitlist but a permanent list.

### 3.2 Being remembered + continuity (the "someone sent me something" emotional core)

36. **Kept On File** — institutional vocabulary turned warm. "We
    kept you on file." Reads as a small romance between an
    institution and a person.
37. **Membership Pending** — a state that never resolves. You are
    pending membership, in some way, always. Delightful.
38. **The Standing Apology** — the constant "sorry, cancelled" that
    is the actual signature of the product. Dry humour; survives a
    re-read.
39. **Cordially, Yours** — the formal signoff turned brand name.
40. **Cordially** — short, one word, dry, warm.
41. **Yours, Cordially** — the salutation re-cast. "Yours, Cordially"
    is the kind of thing a member could imagine seeing in their
    inbox.

### 3.3 Cancellation + relief (the "I can stay home without guilt" emotional core)

42. **Better Plans** — the perpetual "I have better plans". Yours
    always seem to be on.
43. **The Cancel Club** — owns the joke. Warm version.
44. **Plans On Hold** — the gentle version.
45. **Quiet Plans** — gentle + small + warm.
46. **Soft Plans** — gentler still.
47. **The Last-Minute Card** — re-cast; the card arrives at the
    last minute. Survives a re-read.

### 3.4 Standing + status (the "your standing is good" emotional core)

48. **Your Standing** — short, two words, possessive. "Your standing
    is good" / "your standing is preserved".
49. **The Standing Order** — a recurring invitation; also a banking
    term, but the warm version reads first.
50. **Good Standing** — a status that is, in this case, _the
    whole product_.

### 3.5 Mailbox + correspondence (the "I got a thing" emotional core)

51. **The Card Club** — the card is the medium; the club is the
    sender.
52. **On The Post** — being on the post (australian/british for
    "in the mail").
53. **In The Post** — same; the brand name is the inbox state.
54. **Next Post** — the next thing in the post, from the club.

### 3.6 Social choreography (the "my calendar looks alive" emotional core)

55. **Always Off** — calendar setting; "always off, never absent".
    The product makes your absence an enabled state, not a bug.
56. **On Schedule** — your social calendar appears alive.
57. **The Calendar Club** — the calendar is the artefact.
58. **Save The Date** — the phrase gets subverted; a real date
    never quite arrives, but the date is always saved.
59. **Standing Plans** — plans that are always there for you.

### 3.7 Contemporary brand-mark (the "this is a 2026 thing, not a 1906 thing" emotional core)

60. **Cordial** — single word, contemporary, warm. Doubles as a
    typography-friendly mark.
61. **Standing** — single word; the status; the continuity.
62. **Pending** — single word; the always-resolving state.
63. **Kept** — single word; the verb, not the noun.
64. **Off** — single word; the calendar setting.
65. **Plans** — single word; the artefact of belonging.

### 3.8 Slightly surreal / dry (the "I actually want to belong to this" emotional core)

66. **The Apology Society** — re-cast from "Society of Regrettable
    Commitments". Shorter; drier; the constant "sorry" is the point.
67. **The Without Club** — the club of "without" (without attending,
    without performing, without obligation). Slightly absurd.
68. **Plans With You** (kept — also a strong short candidate).
69. **You Were Invited** — past-tense. The invitation already
    happened in some sense; you were on the list before you
    joined.

### 3.9 Warm + personal (the "I actually want to belong to this" emotional core, warmer)

70. **The Kind Membership** — kindness as the product.
71. **The Doubly Sure** — you're definitely on the list, definitely
    remembered. Drier version: "The Doubly Sure Club".
72. **The Returned-To** — you can always return. A small romance.

## 5. SEO and collision check (high-level only, not legal)

This is not a legal trademark clearance. The notes below are
public-collision and SEO-direction observations only. The user must
do formal clearance before locking.

- **"The Reserved Society"** is also the title of several books,
  films, and small businesses. Long-tail winnable on
  "The Reserved Society social club" / "membership".
- **"The Standing Society"** is a historical phrase (post-Restoration
  English political factions). The brand _can_ lean into that
  resonance, but it should be a deliberate decision.
- **"The Standing Date"** is also a financial term. Brand-strategy
  review before lock.
- **"Membership Pending"** is a generic phrase across many
  contexts. SERP collision is a problem; the brand would have to
  win the "Membership Pending social club" / "club" long tail.
- **"Cordially, Yours" / "Cordially" / "Yours, Cordially"** are
  generic phrases. The single word "Cordial" or "Cordially" is
  most distinctive; "Cordially, Yours" is too close to common
  email signoffs to be defensible.
- **"The Cancel Club"** is currently low-competition but reads as
  a meme; needs brand-strategy review.
- **"Plans With You" / "Plans For You"** is low-competition and
  emotionally right; the "Plans With You Club" / "Plans With You
  membership" long tail is clean.
- **"Always Off"** is currently used in product UI ("always-off
  mode"). Brand-strategy review before lock.
- **"The Held Table"** has a small restaurant collision but
  winnable on "The Held Table club / membership / social".
- **"Better Plans"** is a very common phrase. SERP collision is
  too high.
- **"You Were Invited"** is also a film and event series. Brand
  collision risk is real.
- **"Good Standing"** is used by many banks and clubs. Too generic.
- **"On The Post"** is also a children's TV show. Brand
  collision.
- **"Save The Date"** is the standard phrase; brand collision is
  total.

## 6. The five-name shortlist

The five strongest names, judged across emotional fit, contemporary
register, SEO/collision, memorability, flexibility across digital and
physical, and dry-humour potential:

1. **The Reserved Society** (inherited) — the double meaning still
   works on the emotional criteria. "Reserved" = _kept aside for
   you_ + _quiet_, and the product does both. The pun lands
   quietly. The heritage-society aesthetic the previous round
   attached to it is not intrinsic to the name.
2. **Plans With You** (new) — the product in three words. The brand
   is the verb. Short, contemporary, instantly legible. The
   cancellation is built into the name: the plans include you, but
   they are still plans.
3. **The Always-Invited** (new) — you are always invited. The
   kindness is in the _always_. Warm, dry, slightly absurd; the
   kind of name that makes a person think "this is weirdly
   brilliant".
4. **Cordially** (new) — single word, contemporary, warm, soft.
   Doubles as a typography-friendly mark; the word itself does
   the work of the brand voice.
5. **Membership Pending** (new) — a state that never resolves.
   Delightful. The product is, in some sense, _always pending_
   membership — that is, it never feels fully resolved, which is
   the whole point of the product.

### Why these five and not the others

- **The Reserved Society** survives because the _name_ is good. The
  previous round's recommendation of the visual "Modernist
  Institution" direction is a separate decision; the name can
  stand on its own.
- **Plans With You** is the strongest new candidate. It is the
  product in three words and it does not lean on heritage.
- **The Always-Invited** is the warmest of the new candidates and
  the one most likely to make a member think "this is for me".
- **Cordially** is the most contemporary and the most flexible
  brand-mark candidate. Single-word names survive across mediums
  better than two-word names.
- **Membership Pending** is the most distinctive; it is also the
  one that needs the most brand-strategy thinking because of the
  generic-phrase collision.

### Names that did not make the final five

- **The Standing Society** is a strong second; it loses to
  _Plans With You_ on contemporary register but beats it on
  institutional depth. A user who wants the constitutional
  register should pick _The Standing Society_ over the others.
- **The Standing Date** is a strong candidate but loses to
  _The Always-Invited_ on emotional warmth.
- **The Held Table** is a strong candidate but is more of a
  visual concept than a name.
- **The Standing Apology** is the dry-humour winner but risks
  being read as a complaint.
- **The Calendar Club** is the calendar-mechanics winner but reads
  as a SaaS product, not a social club.

### Recommendation (advisory only, not locked)

If the user wants the most contemporary and emotionally direct name
that does not lean on heritage: **Plans With You**.

If the user wants the most brand-flexible single word: **Cordially**.

If the user wants the warmest member-facing name: **The Always-Invited**.

If the user wants the most distinctive, slightly absurd name:
**Membership Pending**.

If the user wants the name that the previous round's heritage
exploration would have locked: **The Reserved Society**, but with
the explicit decision to _not_ attach the heritage aesthetic to it.

The user decides.
