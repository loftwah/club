## Platform

web

## Users

Plans With You serves adults who want the assurance and recognition of membership without a requirement to attend events, reply, network, post, log in frequently, or socially perform. A silent member can remain a successful member.

Operators use the same product to supervise deliberately constructed plans, scheduled cancellations, correspondence, milestones, optional services, failures and human fulfilment.

## Product Purpose

Plans With You creates ordinary social plans for members and then deliberately cancels them. The plan is real enough to create anticipation and inclusion; cancellation is intentional successful fulfilment and releases the member from attendance. The continuing value is a cared-for membership relationship expressed through invitations, cancellations, correspondence, transparent memory, milestones and optional physical or human services.

## Positioning

The product offers belonging without performance: members are included in a real, accumulating institutional relationship while ordinary plans reliably terminate in cancellation rather than attendance.

## Operating Context

- Public visitors learn the mechanism, compare three tiers and join the live waiting list.
- Members access a private record of membership, plans, correspondence, memory, milestones and independently consented optional services.
- Operators supervise exceptions, fulfilment, failures and auditable state-machine transitions.
- Digital and physical outputs include invitations, cancellations, email, letters, cards, gifts and calendar records.
- Chapters may be active or forming; an unsupported location must not be represented as an active chapter without real operational data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public product name: Plans With You.
- Tier names: Member, Corresponding Member and Deluxe Member.
- Displayed prices: A$5, A$20 and A$50. Payments and Stripe activation remain disabled.
- Higher tiers add physical and human fulfilment, never greater belonging.
- Ordinary events are deliberately cancelled. `ATTENDED`, `CHECKED_IN` and `NO_SHOW` are forbidden ordinary-event states. RSVP mechanics must not imply attendance.
- Member memory is transparent and member-controlled. Confirmed facts require provenance; candidate, rejected, revoked, do-not-mention, inspection, editing, deletion and restriction states remain explicit.
- AI may propose facts but may not silently invent confirmed member truth.
- Optional physical and human services require entitlement, permission and prerequisites. Revocation affects future scheduled work.
- D1/runtime owns truth. Agents act through bounded API/MCP capabilities and important actions are auditable and idempotent.
- No false venue partnerships, endorsements, bookings, operating chapters, member counts or business statistics.
- Australian paid-operation and legal constraints do not prevent collecting truthful global waitlist interest.
- Production waitlist data must be preserved. Schema changes must be additive and validated outside production first.
- Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Resend and the existing modular TypeScript/Astro architecture remain the application foundation.

## Brand Commitments

- The governing product world is contemporary scheduling and administrative objects: plans, dates, calendars, registration, state and cancellation.
- Correspondence, paper, mail and private physical attention provide supporting warmth.
- Avoid old-money clubs, law firms, fake aristocracy, Victorian societies, wax-seal repetition, heraldry, beige luxury templates and heritage cosplay.
- The product should feel magical, sophisticated, distinctive, contemporary, tactile, alive, emotionally legible, fast, accessible and trustworthy.
- Visual magic must embody product mechanics rather than decorative WebGL, blobs, meaningless parallax or generic luxury motifs.
- Readable typography remains real HTML/CSS or deterministic print layout, never garbled generated imagery.

## Product Principles

1. Belonging is not conditional on attendance, engagement or social performance.
2. Cancellation is a successful, emotionally legible product outcome.
3. Truth, consent, entitlement and provenance outrank generative convenience.
4. The relationship should visibly accumulate through plans, correspondence, memory and milestones.
5. Rich presentation must remain semantic, fast, accessible and production-operable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Essential information and actions must remain semantic and usable without canvas, WebGL, hover, fine-pointer input or motion. Reduced-motion and reduced-data experiences must be deliberately composed rather than degraded leftovers.
