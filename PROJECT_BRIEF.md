# Project Brief — Social Club

A real paid social club that gives people **belonging, inclusion, continuity and personal attention without requiring attendance or participation**.

The club is real. Membership is real. Correspondence is real. Member history is real. Care is real. Ordinary club events are deliberately constructed experiences that are later cancelled. Members do not attend them.

A member can join, choose what they want, tell the Society what it may remember, receive plausible local invitations, receive cancellations, newsletters, birthday and anniversary recognition, physical correspondence, gifts and calls according to tier and permission, request manufactured commitments, and separately book real-world Society representative/social-role services.

A member never has to post, react, network, maintain a streak, regularly log in, reply, or prove engagement. A completely silent member can be a successful member.

The signature emotional arc is:

```text
I belong
→ I am invited
→ I have plans
→ the plan feels plausible
→ the date approaches
→ the club cancels it
→ relief
→ the relationship continues
```

Cancellation is successful fulfilment, not failure.

## Tiers

- **A$5/month** — automated/core membership, newsletter, event/cancellation experience, personalised digital correspondence, basic real-world touches
- **A$20/month** — materially more physical correspondence and human fulfilment
- **A$50/month** — deluxe gifts, calls, richer personal attention and premium human treatment

Higher tiers buy more expensive physical/human fulfilment, not greater belonging.

## Real-world appearance service

A separate paid service may let members or non-members hire a real person to appear at a real event in a harmless agreed role such as friend, plus-one, club representative, guest or supporter. The customer understands the arrangement. Travel, accommodation, transport and substantial preparation are quoted separately. It is not unlimited labour included in A$50.

## Manufactured commitments

An optional A$20/A$50 feature can create a consensual artificial social obligation to generate useful pressure, for example to clean a home or finish admin. The member knows the scenario is constructed, explicitly confirms each scenario, may abort at any time, and the Society must explicitly cancel/close it. No fictional visitor may remain indefinitely “on the way”.

## Four actors

1. Customer/member
2. Human operator
3. Club runtime/system
4. AI/agent runtime, local or hosted

Cloudflare owns truth. AI and humans act through APIs/MCP and policy.
